A data analytics project aimed at analyzing sales data drawing insights and giving recommendations for making proper business decisions



Data preparation process:
- Loading the data into power BI using powerquery
- checking for null values and removing them
- checking for duplicates and removing them
- removal of outliers

Dax and Table creation:
Since the project involves creating a table and doing some dax and measures, the following were done:
- Calender table for the purpose of making it a date table to do time intelligence(Year-over-year)
- creating measures for value last year.

- Total customers the business has is 793, with more sales coming from the leading customer "Sean Miller"($25k in sales)
- Total Sales made in all the financial years is at $2.30million, profit stands at $286.41K
- Doing a trend analysis on both Sales and Profits, the following were observed:
  1. quantity of goods sold is directly proportional to sales and profits(higher quantities of goods sold will likely yield more sales and profits).
  2. 2011 was the less most-profitable year, the gap between the sales and profits was wider than any other financial year.
  3. Technology gave more sales than Furniture and office supplies categories, Furniture generated disappointing figures in profits considering the fact that it made almost the same figures with office supplies in sales(a large number of sales in furniture ended up as losses).
  4. Los Angeles and New York lead in Sales made all through the financial years, California and New York are the leading states.

RECOMMENDATIONS:
From my analysis of this business data, I recommend the following to help better the business situation:
 - It is observed that some sales ended in losses, but more information would have shed more light to the situation, the following can be done:
    a. Include the date the goods were taking into stock, older goods might cause a depreciation in value, affecting the sales value and cause a loss in place of profits.
    b. If goods are sold at discounted prices, it should be indicated.
